# Privacy Policy

## What we collect

When you register, use the site, or contact us, we may collect your username, email address, IP address, the information you submit, and service logs. We use this information to operate and improve the site, troubleshoot problems, and prevent abuse. Cookies keep you signed in and store some game data. We generally keep cookies private. If you sign up or sign in through a third party, we may exchange information needed for authentication.

## Online play and match replays

In peer-to-peer play, the other player can see your IP address. To keep it private from the other player, turn on Force TURN in your in-game player card.

Ranked Phantasmagoria of Flower View replays are automatically uploaded to our servers so we can verify results and assign ratings fairly. To hide those replays from public view, turn off the game's display in your profile settings. Replays are still uploaded automatically when public display is off.

## Protection and disclosure

We store passwords as hashes. We generally keep email addresses, IP addresses, sign-in credentials, and service logs private. Other people can see information you publish yourself. We may disclose relevant information when required by law or when needed to address attacks, cheating, or other abuse.

## Access and deletion

You can contact us through the site to ask what personal information we hold about you. You can usually request account deletion in your account settings or by contacting an admin. Once we receive a request, we stop collecting that data, set existing records aside, and usually delete or anonymize them within 30 business days. Account deletion may be limited for banned accounts or serious abuse, and relevant records may be kept.

Minors must use the site with a parent or guardian's permission and guidance. Guardians can contact us to ask about their child's data or request deletion of data collected without their permission.

When this policy changes, we may ask you to review and confirm it again.
