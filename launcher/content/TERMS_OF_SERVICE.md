# Terms of Service

Touhou Online is a free, non-commercial service. Maintenance and outages can happen with little notice. Back up your important saves and replays, and if you will, please donate to keep this thing working.

## What we may remove

- Threats, harassment, hate speech, or sharing someone’s private information without permission.
- Sexually explicit material, graphic violence, or encouragement of violence.
- Ads, sales, paid promotions, scams, spam, malicious links, or material shared illegally or without permission.

## When we may invalidate a match

- Someone cheated, used automated inputs, changed the game speed or data, or deliberately exploited a bug.
- Someone played, arranged, or threw a match to manipulate ratings.
- The result was fabricated or impossible, or someone submitted another player’s replay as their own.

## When we may restrict or ban an account

- **Unfair play.** Cheating, fixing matches, or sharing accounts.
- **Multiaccounting.** Using more than one account, for any reason.
- **Harassment.** This includes conduct off the site when it involves the community.
- **Content violations.** Serious or repeated violations, or getting around a filter.
- **Impersonation.** Pretending to be staff. We handle player impersonation case by case.
- **Service abuse.** Deliberately disrupting the service or abusing the report button.

If you’ve lost access to your account, ask an admin before making another.\
If you want to change your name, tell an admin why. We usually approve changes for personal reasons.\
But **DO NOT MAKE ANOTHER ACCOUNT**.

Bans are permanent unless we say otherwise. You can appeal by messaging a moderator directly on Discord.

Report problems on the site when you can. You can also reach moderators and admins on [Discord](https://discord.gg/HwVz9ZTSF).
